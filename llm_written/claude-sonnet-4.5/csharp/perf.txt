=== Performance Results ===

Test Configuration:
  - Graph size: 200 vertices, 40 SCCs
  - System: 16 processors available
  - Test run: Large graph (triggers parallelization)

Timing Results:
  Sequential:  2.25 ms
  Parallel:    1.15 ms
  Speedup:     1.96×

Analysis:
  - Parallelization threshold: 4 SCCs (sequential fallback below this)
  - Worker pool: Bounded to Environment.ProcessorCount (16 cores)
  - The speedup is conservative due to:
    1. Small total work (only 2.25ms sequential)
    2. Task scheduling overhead for 40 SCCs
    3. Each SCC's edge minimization is relatively fast
  - Expected speedup at larger scale (1000+ vertices, 100+ SCCs): 2-4×

Determinism:
  - All parallel runs produce identical output hash
  - Fixed-order merge (index-based array)
